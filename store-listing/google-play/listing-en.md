# Google Play store listing (English)

Translated from the Cafe Bazaar description (2026-11-07), adapted for the Google Play version: the name is Kin Bridge, it speaks 16 languages, and dates use the Persian calendar in Persian and the Gregorian calendar in every other language.

## App name (max 30 characters)

Kin Bridge: Family Tree

## Short description (max 80 characters)

Build your family tree offline. No account; everything stays on your phone.

## Full description (max 4000 characters)

Build your family tree beautifully with Kin Bridge, and keep it forever. Fully offline, with no account or internet needed.

🌳 Made for big families: add as many children, grandchildren and generations as you like, and get a live, zoomable chart of your family's generations.

📐 A neat, readable tree: cards arrange themselves automatically to form your family tree.

📄 PDF for printing the whole family tree on A4 paper with large text, plus a list of everyone in it. Stick the pages together with the help of the guide map and get one big poster.

💾 Your data is never lost: every change is saved automatically. Back up your family trees together with their photos, and share them with others.

📅 Your calendar: enter birth, death and marriage dates in the Persian (Solar Hijri) calendar when the app is in Persian, and in the Gregorian calendar in every other language.

✨ More features:
• Add unlimited people, spouses, parents and children
• Name, surname, nickname, place and date of birth, place and date of death, and notes
• Automatic family tree chart, drawn by generation
• Smooth zooming and panning on the chart, in portrait or landscape
• Find how two people are related, and every way they are connected
• Pick one person and see their ancestors and descendants on their own
• Dark (night) and light (day) mode
• Record several marriages for one person, and show marriages that ended
• Record twins, triplets and more
• Export and import your data as a file, to back it up or move it to another phone
• 16 languages, including right-to-left Persian, Arabic and Urdu
• Keep several separate family trees, for example your father's and your mother's side
• A black ribbon for those who have passed away

Your privacy matters: all of your family's information is stored only on your own phone and is never sent to any server.

Install Kin Bridge now and record your family's story for the generations to come.
